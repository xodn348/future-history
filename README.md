# A Future History of Humanity — forecast from 2026

A data-anchored speculative forecast of the next two centuries (2026 → 2200), told along **three branches** and illustrated era by era. Built as a single static GitHub Pages site.

**Live site:** open `index.html`, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`, root).

## The idea

Not prophecy, not fantasy. Every claim starts from a real 2026 trend — frontier-AI scaling, the solar/battery cost curve, demographic decline, fusion nearing net-gain, the climate bill coming due — and reasons forward. Because the future is not single, it is written as three internally-consistent branches that share the same starting conditions and diverge on the choices we actually control:

- **Ascent** — we navigate it well.
- **Fracture** — strain and stratification.
- **Wildcard** — the discontinuity nobody priced in (an AI-discovered ambient superconductor and the cascade it triggers).

Six eras: **2030, 2050, 2075, 2100, 2150, 2200.** Six drivers per era: AI & cognition · climate & energy · geopolitics & governance · demography & society · biotech & health · space & frontier. Confidence is tagged inline (*likely / plausible / wildcard / remote*), and each era names its **branch point** — the hinge that had to break one way.

The through-line thesis: across every branch, capability is near-certain; what is open is **distribution** — whether the gains get a shared floor or a higher wall.

## Structure

```
index.html        — the full site (self-contained, inline CSS)
images/*.svg       — one generated era illustration per era (code-generated SVG)
content/*.md       — the three full branch timelines (Ascent / Fracture / Wildcard)
```

## How it was built

A multi-agent forecasting pass: three agents each wrote a full branch arc (2026→2200); six agents each generated an era illustration; six more wove the branches into a per-era synthesis ("what's robust / where it forks / what to watch"). The site stitches the results together.

> Era art is **code-generated SVG editorial illustration**, not photographic AI imagery. The forecast is an argument in the form of a history — treat the dates as scaffolding for reasoning, and the forks and leading indicators as the testable part.
