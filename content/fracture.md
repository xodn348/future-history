# The Fracture Scenario

*The conflict-and-strain branch: climate damage compounds faster than adaptation, AI concentrates power and destabilizes labor and information ecosystems, great-power conflict and institutional decay dominate. Resilience, partial recoveries, and thriving pockets remain.*

## 2030 — The Acceleration
The decade opens with compounding shocks rather than a single rupture. Frontier AI capability roughly doubles on ~6-month cadences (the 2024-26 compute-scaling and agentic-coding trend extended), but gains concentrate in ~5 US and Chinese labs and the hyperscalers that own the chips. Knowledge-work displacement outruns reskilling: entry-level coding, paralegal, and back-office roles thin first (likely). Climate delivers its bill early — 2023-26's record ocean heat and El Niño volatility mature into back-to-back failed monsoons and a Gulf/Mediterranean hurricane season that breaks insurance markets (plausible). Synthetic media erodes the shared factual baseline ahead of pivotal elections. Energy demand from datacenters collides with grid limits, and gas plus delayed renewables fill the gap.
**Key events:**
- 2027: First trillion-parameter agent cluster passes sustained autonomous software-engineering benchmarks; layoffs framed as "efficiency." (likely)
- 2028: A major US coastal insurer withdraws from three states; mortgage markets wobble. (plausible)
- 2029: AI-fabricated "October surprise" disputed across two G20 elections; trust in institutions drops sharply. (plausible)
- 2030: Taiwan Strait quarantine scare freezes advanced-chip shipments for 11 weeks. (wildcard)
**Branch point:** Governments treated AI labor displacement and climate-insurance collapse as separate quarterly problems, never building the redistributive buffer that would have absorbed both at once.

## 2050 — The Reckoning
The midcentury inheritance is a world that works, but unevenly and bitterly. Warming passes 1.5°C decisively and tracks toward 2.4-2.7°C; the loss is measured in abandoned districts, not extinction (likely). Roughly 200-400 million people have moved or been displaced by heat, water stress, and coastal failure, and migration has hardened borders everywhere (plausible). AI did not "take all jobs," but it captured the returns: a handful of compute-owning firms and states command outsized wealth, while a precarious majority subsists on patchy basic-income schemes where governments can afford them. Great-power order has fragmented into armed blocs around chips, energy, and rare earths. Yet adaptation is real — desalination, heat-resilient crops, and distributed solar keep India and the Sahel habitable where investment arrived.
**Key events:**
- 2034: A limited resource war over Himalayan watersheds; first AI-coordinated drone swarms in combat. (plausible)
- 2041: Atlantic circulation (AMOC) weakening confirmed; Northern Europe braces for harsher winters. (wildcard)
- 2047: Pandemic from a thawed or engineered pathogen; mRNA platforms contain it in ~14 months. (plausible)
- 2050: Compute-rich city-states formally outcompete several nation-states on GDP. (plausible)
**Branch point:** The 2040 failure to govern AI-compute concentration internationally let private and authoritarian actors privatize the productivity windfall instead of socializing it.

## 2075 — The Divergence
By the third quarter of the century the human story splits cleanly into tiers. Enclaves — climate-buffered, AI-augmented arcologies in Canada, Patagonia, the Nordics, and fortified Gulf cities — run near-post-scarcity: longevity therapeutics push healthy lifespans past 100, and cognition is routinely machine-assisted (plausible). Outside them, a larger world manages chronic scarcity, intermittent states, and 2.5-3°C heat with grit and improvisation. Crucially, the gap is not total collapse: cheap solar, open-weight AI tutors, and mesh networks give even poor regions capabilities a 2026 government would envy (likely). Biotech is the sharpest divide — germline editing and life-extension are enclave luxuries, breeding genuine biological inequality (wildcard). Space is a frontier for the rich: orbital industry and a permanent lunar base exist, but as elite infrastructure, not escape hatches.
**Key events:**
- 2058: First clinically validated multi-decade lifespan extension; access splits by wealth. (plausible)
- 2063: A federation of autonomous AI-run city-regions secedes from a failing state. (wildcard)
- 2071: Lunar helium-3 / industrial base declared operational by a corporate-state consortium. (plausible)
- 2075: Open-weight "good-enough" AGI proliferates, narrowing the tool gap even as the wealth gap widens. (likely)
**Branch point:** Enclaves chose fortification over integration around 2060 — exporting tools but hoarding longevity and capital, locking divergence into the species.

## 2100 — The New Equilibrium
The century closes not in apocalypse but in a hardened, multi-speed civilization that has stopped expecting things to get better fast. Warming plateaus near 2.5-3°C as emissions finally hit net-zero and early carbon-removal bends the curve; the climate is stable but degraded, and a band of the tropics is seasonally uninhabitable, governed remotely or abandoned (plausible). Global population peaks below 9.5 billion and begins falling, easing some resource pressure while aging strains every economy (likely). Governance has settled into a patchwork: AI-administered enclave-states, surviving democracies, and machine-mediated authoritarian blocs coexist in uneasy, armed equilibrium. Superintelligence, where it exists, is corralled by deterrence and mutual dependence rather than alignment guarantees — an unstable peace that has held (wildcard). The thriving pockets are genuinely humane; the cost is that "humanity" no longer names one shared condition.
**Key events:**
- 2083: Net-zero reached globally, two decades late; removal infrastructure scales. (plausible)
- 2089: First treaty governing autonomous AI states and their compute. (plausible)
- 2094: Self-sustaining Mars settlement of ~1,000 — symbolic, not a lifeboat. (wildcard)
- 2100: Global population peaks and turns down. (likely)
**Branch point:** The decision to manage rather than reverse divergence — institutionalizing tiers in the 2089 treaty — made stratification permanent instead of transitional.

## 2150 — Beyond the Cradle
A century and a half out, humanity is durable, plural, and quietly post-human at its frontier. Earth supports perhaps 7-8 billion people across radically unequal modes of life: AI-managed abundance for enclave populations and their descendants, including genetically and cybernetically modified lineages, alongside vast regions living modestly but stably with mature tech (plausible). Off-world, several million people inhabit lunar, Martian, and orbital habitats — economically real now, no longer symbolic (wildcard). The deepest fracture has become biological and cognitive: augmented and baseline humans diverge in lifespan, capability, and even perception, raising whether they remain one species (wildcard). Yet the dark branch never closed the future — there is no extinction, no singleton tyranny, no dead Earth. Resilience won a narrower victory than the optimists hoped: survival and continued progress, purchased at the price of unity and justice.
**Key events:**
- 2118: First multi-generational space-born population reaches adulthood off Earth. (plausible)
- 2127: Legal recognition of augmented humans as a distinct rights category in several polities. (wildcard)
- 2140: Climate restoration projects begin slowly lowering global temperature. (plausible)
- 2150: Off-world population surpasses several million. (wildcard)
**Branch point:** Letting biological augmentation diffuse without any shared floor, after 2120, turned inequality from economic into hereditary and bodily — the fracture made flesh.

## 2200 — The Long Result
Two centuries from 2026, the Fracture branch resolves into something sober and lasting: a fragmented but enduring civilization spanning Earth and near-space, having passed through its hardest filter without breaking and without healing. Climate is slowly being engineered back toward preindustrial norms over coming centuries; the tropics begin re-greening at the edges (plausible). Descendant populations are genuinely diverse — baseline humans, augmented lineages, AI-integrated minds, and space-adapted variants — coexisting in a loose, often tense order held together by trade, deterrence, and exhaustion with conflict rather than by justice or shared identity (wildcard). The thriving pockets are extraordinary: longevity, post-scarcity, expanding presence across the solar system. But the long result of choosing fortification over solidarity at every fork is permanence — stratification encoded into biology, geography, and law, a stable inequality that no one any longer expects to dissolve (plausible).
**Key events:**
- 2165: Solar-system-wide communication and trade network stabilizes across human settlements. (plausible)
- 2180: First deliberate large-scale climate-restoration milestone met. (plausible)
- 2195: Augmented and baseline lineages formalize coexistence frameworks. (wildcard)
- 2200: Human and post-human population across all habitats stabilizes near 9 billion. (plausible)
**Branch point:** The cumulative refusal, across six generations, to build a shared floor — ecological, economic, or biological — is what made the fracture the permanent shape of the long result.
